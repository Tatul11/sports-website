Sadyr Japarov attends opening of World Urban Forum

Local • 18 May • 09:42 Bishkek time

Presidents of Kyrgyzstan, Azerbaijan, and other leaders at the opening ceremony of the World Urban Forum. / Photo: Press Service of the President of the Republic of Kyrgyzstan.

President of the Republic of Kyrgyzstan Sadyr Japarov participated in the opening ceremony of the World Urban Forum on May 18 in Baku, Azerbaijan.

According to the press service of the President, during his visit, the head of our state also held a meeting with the President of the Republic of Azerbaijan, Ilham Aliyev.

A wide representation of the world community was observed.

The theme of the current forum is "Investing in Urban Future". The event is being attended by heads of state and government, representatives of international organizations and experts.

During the meeting, the leaders discussed issues of further enhancing multifaceted cooperation and practical interaction, as well as the implementation of joint projects in the fields of industry, energy, agriculture, and transport.

The President of Kyrgyzstan will also deliver a speech at the plenary session of the forum later today.

Source: Official Press Release
